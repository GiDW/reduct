# Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-07

Reduct processes files locally on your PC. It collects, stores and sends no personal data.

Reduct makes no network connections of its own. The only exception is when you click one of its Microsoft Store buttons: Windows then opens the Store page for the HEVC Video Extensions. Your settings and any failure logs are stored locally, in Reduct's own app data folder on your PC, and never leave it.

Questions: open an issue at <https://github.com/GiDW/reduct/issues>.
